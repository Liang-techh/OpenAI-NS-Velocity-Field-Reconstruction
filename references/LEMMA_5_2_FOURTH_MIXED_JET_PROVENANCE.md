## Executable mapping

`src/openai_ns_reconstruction/background_moment_repair_fourth_mixed_jets.py` lifts the already-landed function-level Lemma 5.2 repair by exactly the three total-order-four U derivatives required by `background_regular_flux_third_mixed_jets.py`:

- `U_XXetaeta`,
- `U_Xetaetaeta`, and
- `U_etaetaetaeta`.

For the compact correction

`Delta U_n(X,eta) = sum_j alpha_j(eta) b_j(sqrt(2X))`,

the new fields are differentiated analytically as

- `partial_X^2 partial_eta^2 Delta U_n = sum_j alpha_j'' partial_X^2 b_j`,
- `partial_X partial_eta^3 Delta U_n = sum_j alpha_j''' partial_X b_j`, and
- `partial_eta^4 Delta U_n = sum_j alpha_j'''' b_j`.

The radial derivatives reuse the exact landed `R=sqrt(2X)` compact-bump jet.  The eta derivatives come from `solve_lemma52_eta_jets`, which propagates arbitrary finite ordinary eta jets through the fixed moment matrices and the Eq. (5.16) quotient/product identities.  Production performs no neighboring-eta finite difference and no sampled `V/X` division.

The adapter corrects all lower fields from the same alpha jet and returns a coherent `AxialFourthMixedJet`.  Its `beta_third_mixed_jet` method then feeds that repaired U jet directly into the landed analytic Eq. (5.2) third-mixed regular-flux map.

Because every repair bump is C-infinity and supported on a strict positive-radius interval, points off all repair supports return the unrepaired fourth-mixed jet exactly and do not request unavailable fourth eta moment/patch rows.

## Independent regression

`tests/test_background_moment_repair_fourth_mixed_jets.py` uses degree-four analytic moment functions and a constant nonzero patch factor.  The production fourth-mixed repair is checked against an independent centered eta difference of the previously landed repaired third-mixed adapter:

- `d_eta(U_XXeta)` against `U_XXetaeta`,
- `d_eta(U_Xetaeta)` against `U_Xetaetaeta`, and
- `d_eta(U_etaetaeta)` against `U_etaetaetaeta`.

The regression also verifies that the first nine U-jet fields agree with the landed third-mixed repair, that the repaired fourth-mixed jet feeds the Eq. (5.2) beta third-mixed adapter and preserves its function-level beta value, and that off-support evaluation skips unneeded high eta rows while active-support missing rows fail closed.  A non-`AxialFourthMixedJet` base provider is rejected.
